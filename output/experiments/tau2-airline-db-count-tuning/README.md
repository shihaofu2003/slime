# tau2-airline-db-count-tuning

Purpose: tune asynchronous DB-count on Airline before another four-domain run. Start from full-domain SFT iter4673 with independent optimizers; compare learning rates under a common larger batch. The first pilots stopped after one optimizer update each. Replacement jobs 19999 / 20000 were submitted on 2026-09-16 with batch 64 and unlimited policy lag; job 19999 has completed training and SFT/iter29 evaluation; job 20000 continues training toward its automatic iter29 evaluation.

## Evidence and scope

- [AReaL paper, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22607v3): Airline batch-size ablations favor 512 over 256; 30B-A3B RL uses constant LR 5e-6 and effective batch 128–512. The paper excludes all-success and all-failure groups and uses group-relative advantages.
- [Current 8B example](https://github.com/areal-project/AReaL/blob/main/examples/tau2/config_8b_airline.yaml): LR 1.7e-5, batch 30×8=240, clip 0.4, head off-policy limit 2, 50 interaction steps. Its batch advantage normalization and [all-success-only filter](https://github.com/areal-project/AReaL/blob/main/examples/tau2/train.py) differ from the paper. It starts from a thinking Qwen3-8B; this experiment keeps our non-thinking 4B SFT and User fixed.
- Previous four-domain arms used batch 40, LR 2e-6 and unbounded lag. These pilots change domain, batch and lag together, so they do not independently estimate a batch-size or lag effect. The two pilots differ only in LR.
- Current tail diagnostics showed finite gradients and little TIS clipping; paused Airline credit diagnostics (423 groups / 3,384 trajectories) have response-token-weighted outcome RMS 0.948, progress RMS 0.979 and 7.01% combined-advantage sign flips versus outcome; a flat accepted-group mean alone does not establish a failed optimizer. Selection requires unfiltered reward and matched evaluation.

## Measurements and evaluation

Each job checks every 60 seconds and writes `plots/<run-name>/reward_prefilter.{csv,png}` and `reward_curves.{csv,png}`. Before filtering: all dumped final attempts, including rejected all-zero/all-one groups and unsampleable groups; intermediate retry attempts are not dumped. After filtering: exactly 128 accepted trajectories per completed optimizer update. CSVs retain denominators, 400-trajectory unfiltered smoothing and 10-update accepted smoothing. Short incomplete groups can appear in the unfiltered curve while still awaiting their remaining trajectories.

The planned post-training evaluations were SFT4673 and both iter29 models. They did not run because training was stopped. The protocol is the same official Airline test split (20 tasks × 4 trials = 80 trajectories/model), seed300, temperature 0.6, top-p 1, max_tokens 1,200, max_steps 200, and fixed Qwen3.6 User. Report pass@1, pass@4(any), pass^4 and task-paired uncertainty. These are exploratory comparisons; confirm a selected recipe with an additional seed before claiming a reliable improvement. No diagnostic threshold automatically stops these pilots.

Next round depends on these results: run vanilla with the selected common settings, then vary credit weight; do not change LR, batch, clip and credit weight simultaneously within a comparison. Thirty updates are a pilot, not proof that the recipe works.

A subsequent batch reduction from 128 to 64 can shorten feedback cycles if necessary. Compare credit weight or PPO clip only after collecting enough actual updates; the present data do not justify increasing LR.
